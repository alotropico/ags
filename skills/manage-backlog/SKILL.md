# Manage a backlog

A backlog is inactive future inventory, not a second specification and not an instruction surface. Use the repository's existing backlog or deferred register; do not create another when one already exists.

## Triage

For each candidate:

1. Search active guidance, specifications, plans, the existing backlog, and completed history for an equivalent item.
2. Drop trivia, duplicates, superseded ideas, and negative inventory whose only content is "do not do this now".
3. If the item is already committed active work, move it to its normative or execution home instead of duplicating it here.
4. Preserve it only when future reconsideration could change a useful decision.

## Entry contract

Each retained entry states:

- the proposal;
- its likely home if activated;
- why it is deferred now; and
- the recurring failure, deliberate preference, dependency, or evidence that would justify reconsideration.

Group entries by likely future owner rather than by the conversation that produced them. Merge overlapping entries and preserve concrete evidence or provenance when it will matter later.

## Boundaries

- Recording an item does not authorize implementation, discovery, installation, or loading during ordinary work.
- Do not preserve speculative supporting designs before the candidate is active.
- When evidence promotes a candidate, remove its deferred status and reconcile the specification, plan, ledger, and active material that now own it.
- Report what was added, merged, dropped, promoted, or left unresolved.
